PART 1 - GENERAL
1.1 SUMMARY
A. Section includes four (4) single-module static uninterruptible power supply (UPS) units, tags UPS-A to UPS-D, each feeding one data hall power block, as scheduled on Drawing E-601.
1.2 SUBMITTALS
A. Product Data: Manufacturer's catalog data for each UPS, including electrical ratings, efficiency, heat dissipation, dimensions and weights, and one-line diagram.
B. Where product data covers more than one voltage or frequency, mark the configuration supplied for this project.
PART 2 - PRODUCTS
2.1 UPS MODULE
A. Rating: Each UPS shall have a continuous output rating of not less than 1200 kVA and 1200 kW at unity power factor, at 480 volts.
B. Ambient Rating: The full rating in paragraph A shall be available at an ambient temperature of 40 degrees C.
C. System Voltage: Input, bypass and output shall be 480 volts, 3-phase, 3-wire plus ground, 60 Hz.
D. Topology: Double-conversion (online) design, classified VFI under IEC 62040-3.
E. Efficiency: Double-conversion AC-to-AC efficiency shall be not less than 96 percent at 100 percent load.
F. Input Characteristics: Input power factor shall be not less than 0.99 and input current distortion (THDi) shall not exceed 5 percent at full load.
G. Output Voltage Regulation: Steady-state output voltage shall be held within plus or minus 1 percent for balanced load changes from 0 to 100 percent.
H. Overload: The inverter shall carry 125 percent of rated load for not less than 10 minutes.
I. Static Bypass: Provide an automatic static bypass switch within the UPS.
J. Maintenance Bypass: Provide a manual maintenance bypass, furnished with the UPS.
K. Batteries: Valve-regulated lead-acid or lithium-ion, sized for not less than 10 minutes at full load.
L. Paralleling: The UPS shall be capable of operating in parallel with at least 3 additional units of the same rating.
M. Listing: The UPS shall be listed to UL 1778.
N. Seismic: The UPS shall be certified for seismic installation in accordance with the International Building Code.
2.2 PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS
A. Dimensions: Each UPS shall fit within 110 inches wide, 40 inches deep and 84 inches high.
B. Heat Dissipation: Heat released at full load shall not exceed 150,000 BTU per hour.
PART 3 - EXECUTION
3.1 INSTALLATION
A. Install the UPS in accordance with the manufacturer's written instructions.
